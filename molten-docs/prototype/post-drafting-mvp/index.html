Three ways to shape the editor

Same data, same behaviour, three layouts. Each one is clickable: write an idea, generate, edit, regenerate, copy, delete. Edits carry across all three in this browser tab.

Approach 1

The Notebook

The list and the editor are separate pages. The editor reads top to bottom: your idea, then LinkedIn, X and Bluesky stacked like sections of a document.

  • Calmest, and closest to Ink & Paper
  • Mobile works with no extra layout
  • Comparing drafts means scrolling
Open the Notebook →
Approach 2

The Desk

Works like a notes app. Your posts stay on the left, your idea in the middle, and one draft at a time on the right, in tabs that show each draft's status.

  • Switch between posts instantly
  • Your idea stays visible while you edit a draft
  • Only one draft visible at a time
Open the Desk →
Approach 3

Compose & Spread

Home is a big writing box with your recent posts below it. Generate opens a spread of three columns that all write at once, with your idea folded into a bar at the top.

  • The strongest "three drafts at once" moment
  • No blank page: the empty state is the prompt
  • Busiest, and narrow columns make LinkedIn long
Open the Spread →
Chosen direction · Approach 4

Hybrid: compose first, then the Notebook

Home is the Spread's writing box with recent posts below. Generate opens the Notebook's vertical editor: your idea at the top, then LinkedIn, X and Bluesky stacked, with a status strip.

Open the Hybrid →
