Why you're not posting

It isn't a lack of ideas. It's the pressure to write the perfect post — and the fear of sounding like everyone else when you try.

Your notes app is full of half-thoughts. You open LinkedIn, stare at the box, and close it. When you try ChatGPT, it hands back something with a hook, three emoji bullets and a question at the end. It doesn't sound like you. It sounds like a bot. So you post nothing.

Meanwhile, the prospects who'd hire you never see how you think. The feed is full of AI slop. Your thinking isn't — it just needs a way out of your head.

How it works

Say it. Read it back as you. Post it.

  1. 01

    Say it out loud

    Record a rough voice note between calls or on a walk. It doesn't need to be good. It just needs to be what you actually think.

  2. 02

    Read it back as you

    Levercast drafts a LinkedIn or X post from your own words and phrasing — no generic hooks, no engagement bait, no "As an expert…".

  3. 03

    Approve, then post

    Edit anything, bin anything. Nothing goes out until you say so. You're the author; Levercast stays in the background.
